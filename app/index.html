How we checked every deal

  1. Read the price at the store. Each price was read in a deals roundup that names the store and links to the product there — TechSpot, Redmond Pie, DealNews and SoapCentral's streaming roundup — on 26–27 November 2026.
  2. Did the maths. The discount on every card is computed from the two prices shown, so the percentage always matches.
  3. Gave an honest verdict. New low means the lowest price tracked for that product. Strong price means a genuine cut on something worth buying. Routine saving means a real discount, but a small one — included so you can see what is not a bargain.
  4. Linked to the store. “View at the store” opens the store's own search for that exact product, so you can confirm the price before you buy. Prices can change — if the store shows a different price, trust the store.
